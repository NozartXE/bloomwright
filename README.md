BLOOMWRIGHT — a garden-routing puzzle. Nia, keeper of the Last Spring, turns carved channel tiles to route living water across three terraces and wake twelve gardens in the order they call, chaining a Harmony multiplier before sundown — while Vessk, the Salt Duchess, crusts the stones with salt and, in the two-phase finale, opens her own salt spring and cracks the main channel. Rotate tiles, patch cracks with limited clay, wash the Salt Heart. Deterministic: fixed-step flow simulation, seeded visuals only, every garden proven solvable by the bundled verifier (tools/verify-solutions.mjs).
